# Research: DECOROLALA H3 Live Validation

## H3 reference capacity

**Decision**: Use five ordered images in one H3 full-reference request.

**Rationale**: Live `object_info` reports `reference_images.image_1` through `image_9`. The five
approved sources have distinct roles that cannot be represented honestly by the previous two-image
graph.

**Alternatives considered**: Two-image selection would discard product or identity evidence.
Compositing all references into one image would blur semantic roles and introduce another asset.

## Reference order

**Decision**: Image 1 scene, Image 2 product, Image 3 Lady LaLa full body, Image 4 face, Image 5
rear/side.

**Rationale**: This follows the source production plan and lets the H3 prompt name every reference
unambiguously.

## Prompt mode

**Decision**: Use H3 full-reference six-section English schema with five timed shots, no narration,
dialogue, generated text, or logo; retain ambience and non-diegetic instrumental music.

**Rationale**: References provide reusable scene/product/character subjects rather than an exact
first frame. The structured prompt preserves retention and audio relationships better than a flat
positive prompt.

## Execution boundary

**Decision**: Treat login/USD 10 as prerequisites and require action-time confirmation after the
exact zero-call preview.

**Rationale**: Funding is not permission for an unspecified charge. Provider pricing may change
and is not claimed unless presented for this request.

## Product phase gate

**Decision**: Require a playable retained artifact plus owner `PASS` or `RISK_ACCEPTED` before
Phase 1 Project/Asset development.

**Rationale**: Reference-conditioned product/character continuity remains the dominant feasibility
risk.

## Short validation duration

**Decision**: Use 4 seconds, not the requested 2 seconds, and keep the 15-second workflow disabled.

**Rationale**: Live `object_info` for `MinimaxHailuo03ReferenceNode` reports integer duration
`min: 4`, `max: 15`. Four seconds is therefore the shortest executable H3 request. At the installed
768P badge rate of `$0.1287/second`, the local estimate is `$0.5148`.

**Scope reduction**: Use one continuous product-led tableau with minimal breathing, gaze, fire
flicker, and slow optical push. Walking, glass placement, sitting, multiple cuts, and the full ad
narrative are excluded because they cannot be evaluated honestly in four seconds.
