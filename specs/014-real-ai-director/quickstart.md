# Quickstart and Acceptance

1. Apply migrations to the isolated test database.
2. Open an approved Storyboard and choose Fake AI Director with maximum three shots.
3. Review references, Preview, confirm zero-call, and queue one run.
4. Wait for COMPLETED and verify head/approval are unchanged.
5. Compare/edit, reject one proposal, create another, then adopt with current `If-Match`.
6. Repeat adoption from a stale tab and verify a recoverable conflict.
7. Verify ledgers: CodexManager 0, OpenAI 0, ComfyUI 0, video generation 0.

LIVE acceptance is deliberately not part of completion.
